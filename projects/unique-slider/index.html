Brief

A self-initiated study: show a run of photographs on one page and move through them without dumping every image into a grid.

What was done

The earlier slider was a thin strip of pictures. This version gives the current photo the screen, keeps previous and next obvious, and adds a thumbnail row. It does not autoplay.

The photographs are the same set used in the original slider study.
