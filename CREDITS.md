The reference photograph Dale supplied was not used: its licence could not be confirmed, and it appeared to be a stock image.

No Unsplash or Pexels photograph in this round matched the required harbour-and-Sky-Tower view at night at a usable resolution, so both files were taken from Wikimedia Commons.

The two photographs share a Devonport / Waitematā Harbour vantage looking back at the Sky Tower, but they are not a pixel-identical pair, so the day/night crossfade is a blend rather than a seamless morph. The night original is 1600×600 pixels, so the 2× desktop assets top out at 1472px wide and can look a little soft on a large display.
