# Photo credits

The Auckland CBD waterfront photographs on the homepage replace the earlier SVG skyline illustration. Both were cropped, and the original sky was faded so the photographs melt into the page sky. Daytime clouds are cut from a photograph (the sky punched out, with a soft edge feather) and are not drawn shapes. The moon texture is a cropped full-moon photograph. Required attribution also appears in the site footer.

- **Author:** [DXR](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:DXR) (Daniel Vorndran)
- **Source:** Wikimedia Commons, own work, 28 January 2010
- **Licence:** [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- **Original resolution:** 3872 × 2178. After a centred crop the usable width is 3480px. Derivatives are served at 1280, 1920, 2560, and 3480 (AVIF and WebP; JPEG fallback at 1280, 1920, and 2560). Nothing is upscaled past 3480, so a 2560px viewport at 2× (5120) or 3× cannot be filled from this source.

## Night skyline

- **File:** [Auckland Skyline 383495.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Auckland_Skyline_383495.jpg)
- **Author:** [Ssiyamalan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Ssiyamalan)
- **Source:** Wikimedia Commons, own work. View of the Auckland CBD and Sky Tower across the Waitematā Harbour at dusk, with the city lights on and water in the foreground.
- **Licence:** [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- **Original resolution:** 7830 × 5223. A vertical crop keeps the full 7830px width. Derivatives are served at 1280, 1920, 2560, 3840, and 5120 (AVIF and WebP; JPEG fallback at 1280, 1920, and 2560). 5120 is the largest file, covers 2560 at 2×, and is never an upscale of the 7830 source. 2560 at 3× is not covered, to keep the download small.

The earlier night photograph by 111 Emergency (1600 × 600, CC BY 2.0) was replaced because it was soft on large and high-density screens.

The two skyline photographs share a Waitematā Harbour vantage looking back at the Sky Tower, but they are not a pixel-identical pair, so the day/night crossfade is a blend rather than a seamless morph.

The reference photograph Dale supplied was not used: its licence could not be confirmed, and it appeared to be a stock image.
