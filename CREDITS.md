# Photo credits

The Auckland CBD waterfront photographs on the homepage replace the earlier SVG skyline illustration. Both were cropped, and the sky was cut out along the building silhouette so the photographs sit on the page’s own sky. The daytime sky is a real photograph, with additional cut-out cloud photographs of different shapes drifting in front of it. The moon texture is a cropped full-moon photograph. Required attribution also appears in the site footer.

## Day skyline

- **File:** [Auckland Skyline as seen from Devonport 20100128 3.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Auckland_Skyline_as_seen_from_Devonport_20100128_3.jpg)
- **Author:** [DXR](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:DXR) (Daniel Vorndran)
- **Source:** Wikimedia Commons, own work, 28 January 2010
- **Licence:** [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- **Original resolution:** 3872 × 2178. After a centred crop the usable width is 3480px. Derivatives are served at 1280, 1920, 2560, and 3480 (AVIF and WebP; JPEG fallback at 1280, 1920, and 2560). Nothing is upscaled past 3480, so a 2560px viewport at 2× (5120) or 3× cannot be filled from this source. The sky behind the buildings was removed (a cut-out along the roofline, Sky Tower mast and cranes, with a 1px anti-aliased edge and the sky colour defringed) and the files are served as transparent AVIF and WebP.

## Night skyline

- **File:** [Auckland Skyline 383495.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Auckland_Skyline_383495.jpg)
- **Author:** [Ssiyamalan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Ssiyamalan)
- **Source:** Wikimedia Commons, own work. View of the Auckland CBD and Sky Tower across the Waitematā Harbour at dusk, with the city lights on and water in the foreground.
- **Licence:** [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- **Original resolution:** 7830 × 5223. A vertical crop keeps the full 7830px width. Derivatives are served at 1280, 1920, 2560, 3840, and 5120 (AVIF and WebP; JPEG fallback at 1280, 1920, and 2560). 5120 is the largest file, covers 2560 at 2×, and is never an upscale of the 7830 source. 2560 at 3× is not covered, to keep the download small. The dusk sky was cut out along the silhouette (mast, spire, cranes and rooftops kept, with a 1px anti-aliased edge; a faint glow is left around lit windows). Lit water and its reflections are kept. Files are served as transparent AVIF and WebP.

The earlier night photograph by 111 Emergency (1600 × 600, CC BY 2.0) was replaced because it was soft on large and high-density screens.

The two skyline photographs share a Waitematā Harbour vantage looking back at the Sky Tower, but they are not a pixel-identical pair, so the day/night crossfade is a blend rather than a seamless morph.

The reference photograph Dale supplied was not used: its licence could not be confirmed, and it appeared to be a stock image.

## Daytime clouds

Additional cut-out cloud photographs (the sky punched out, highlights lifted so the sunlit tops read white, with a soft edge-distance feather that falls to fully transparent well inside each file’s bounding box) are placed at varied sizes and densities down the page and drift at different speeds.

- **File:** [Cumulus Clouds on Horizon.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cumulus_Clouds_on_Horizon.jpg)
- **Author:** [Defi22](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Defi22)
- **Source:** Wikimedia Commons, own work, 2 July 2017
- **Licence:** [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- **Original resolution:** 4928 × 3264. Wispy streaks were cut from the upper sky of this photograph.

Further cloud layers were cut from the Rise DHMN day-sky photograph named above (CC BY 4.0), so that large puffs, smaller puffs and wisps are all represented. Served as AVIF and WebP at the widths each cut-out supports, never upscaled.
