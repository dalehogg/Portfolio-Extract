Questions

Before you write

How long does a website take?

A landing page is often ready in a couple of weeks once I have your words and pictures. A business site is often three to six weeks. A custom build depends on the scope we agree first. The timing goes in the quote.

How do revisions work?

The quote includes time for you to review the design and the build and ask for changes. I will say how many review rounds are included before you agree. Work outside that scope is quoted separately.

Do you host the site?

I can launch onto hosting you already pay for, or help you choose a host. Hosting is billed by the host. It is not folded into the starting prices unless the quote says so.

Who owns the site?

You do. After the final invoice is paid, the design, the pages, and the code are yours, and I hand over the files.

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of the business, who the site is for, and any logo, photos, or text you already have. If the words are not ready, we can start from a conversation.

How do payments work?

I send a fixed quote for the agreed scope before I start. The quote sets out when payment is due. I do not publish one price that fits every project.
